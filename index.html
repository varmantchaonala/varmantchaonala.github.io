PhD Student — Quantum Computing & Generative AI

University of Oldenburg, Germany  ·  Faculty V, School of Mathematics and Science 🏅 DAAD Scholar 2024–2027

I work at the intersection of quantum computing and artificial intelligence, designing generative models and language understanding systems grounded in quantum mechanical principles. My research spans quantum transformer architectures, hybrid classical–quantum pipelines, and multimodal quantum generation — evaluated on NISQ-era simulators with Qiskit and Pennylane.

A distinctive thread of my work is Quantum NLP for African low-resource languages: applying quantum information theory to develop small-data-friendly models where conventional AI still falls short. Across all projects, I prioritize interpretability and failure modes alongside benchmark performance.

Themes & Projects

Research

⚛️
Quantum Natural Language Processing
Quantum-native compositional semantics, context-sensitive word embeddings, and scalable QNLP architectures. Focus on African low-resource languages — where data-efficient quantum methods have real potential (DAAD-funded).
🔄
Quantum Generative Modeling
Quantum encoder–decoder architectures with quantum attention, evaluated on Qiskit and Pennylane simulators. Targeting multimodal generation across text, audio, and image modalities.
🔗
Hybrid Classical–Quantum Pipelines
Parameterized quantum circuits (PQC) integrated with classical neural networks under NISQ-era constraints. Focus on representation capacity, gradient stability, and generalization bounds.
🔬
Quantum Algorithms & Optimization
QAOA-based approaches to NP-hard problems including SAT (M.Sc. thesis), QUBO formulations, and quantum ML primitives: SVM, clustering, regression on near-term devices.

Quantum Attention Mechanism

At the core of the quantum transformer is a parameterized circuit encoding token representations into quantum states. Entangling gates create attention-like correlations within the full Hilbert space — something classical dot-product attention can only approximate.
