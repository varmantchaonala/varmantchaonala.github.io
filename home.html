University of Oldenburg, Germany  ·  Faculty V, School of Mathematics and Science 🏅 DAAD Scholar 2024–2027

I work at the intersection of quantum computing and artificial intelligence, designing generative models and language understanding systems grounded in quantum mechanical principles. My research spans quantum transformer architectures, hybrid classical–quantum pipelines, and multimodal quantum generation — evaluated on NISQ-era simulators with Qiskit and Pennylane.

A distinctive thread of my work is Quantum NLP for African low-resource languages: applying quantum information theory to develop small-data-friendly models where conventional AI still falls short. Across all projects, I prioritize interpretability and failure modes alongside benchmark performance.
