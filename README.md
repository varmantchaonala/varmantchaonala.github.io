## 🌍 Why Africa + Quantum AI?

Africa represents one of the most important frontiers for next-generation AI systems. Many challenges across the continent involve:

- **limited computational resources** → quantum efficiency advantages matter
- **low-resource languages** → 2,000+ languages, most with no NLP tools
- **sparse datasets** → quantum data-efficiency is a genuine advantage
- **hard optimization problems** → energy, agriculture, healthcare logistics
- **infrastructure constraints** → lightweight, hybrid systems are essential

These are precisely the conditions where **hybrid quantum-classical approaches** may become most impactful — not in theory, but in practice.
